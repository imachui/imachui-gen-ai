Privacy Policy

Imachui Gen AI · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Imachui Gen AI ("the app") is an AI chat assistant. This page explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

What we collect

  • Your chat messages, which are sent to AI providers to write replies.
  • Memory (facts you ask the app to remember). It is stored on your own device and sent with your chats. It is not saved on our server.
  • An anonymous session ID stored in your browser, used to remember which plugins you installed.
  • Plugin settings (which plugins are installed, on or off, and permissions you chose), stored on our server under that session ID.
  • Feedback you send with the thumbs up or down buttons.

Google and other account connections

If you choose to connect Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, YouTube, GitHub, Notion, or Microsoft, the app asks for read-only access. It never sends email, edits, or deletes anything in your accounts.

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on our server, only so the app can read your data when you ask.
  • When you ask a question that needs your data (for example "what are my unread emails?"), the app reads only what is needed (such as sender, subject, date, and a short preview) and sends that text to an AI provider to write the answer.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or train AI models with it.
  • You can disconnect any plugin at any time in Plugins. This deletes the stored token from our server. You can also remove access in your Google account settings.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party AI providers

Your messages, and any text the app reads from your connected accounts or pasted links to answer you, are sent to AI and search providers (such as Groq, Mistral, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Cloudflare, and Serper). Their own policies apply to how they handle that data. If you add your own API key, your chats go to that provider and the key is stored encrypted on our server.

Retention and deletion

Plugin settings and tokens stay until you disconnect or uninstall the plugin. To ask for all data linked to you to be deleted, email the address below.
